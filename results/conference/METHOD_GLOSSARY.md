# Method glossary

| Acronym | Paper label | Algorithm role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CON | Original route | Principal baseline |
| MWR | Mean-wait repair | Principal baseline |
| QBR | Quantile repair | Principal baseline |
| FSPC | Fixed-station repair | Additional scientific control |
| SLS | Scenario search | Additional scientific control |
| STaR | STaR | Proposed Slack-Targeted Stochastic Charging Repair |

FSPC adjusts partial-charge quantities on the source station topology. SLS is scenario-based local search. CON is the unchanged constructive plan. MWR uses mean waits and QBR uses per-visit quantiles.

NPC, NSP, NAS and NPP respectively remove partial charging, slack prioritization, analytical screening and progressive evaluation. AS1 and FT-SAA are Phase 6 supplementary methods. Archive key `c64` is the 64-plan budget.
