Panel (b) shows paired route-weighted STaR minus each benchmark in percentage points with pointwise 95% family-stratified parent-cluster intervals: CON +16.35 [+14.57, +18.12]; MWR +2.02 [+1.60, +2.43]; QBR +2.10 [+1.36, +2.91]. All three contrasts are exploratory. The registered confirmatory contrast is disclosed only in ARCHIVAL_EVIDENCE.md.

## Fig03_charging_complexity

Panel (b) shows remaining STaR Wilson nonattainment rates 0.25%, 2.52%, 17.74% with denominators 4/1,587, 4/159, 102/575. Routes with 3+ visits account for 102 of 110 remaining nonattainments. ## Fig04_progressive_evaluation

Component illustration on the same 2,321 holdout routes. Horizontal bars compare mean incremental Monte Carlo search draws for full STaR (13,024) and STaR without progressive prefix evaluation (18,927). Progressive evaluation saved approximately 5,903 draws (31.2% relative to NPP). Per-route reliability, attainment and completion are identical for STaR and STaR−NPP in the archived records. This establishes observed simulation-work savings, not elapsed wall-time speedup or equivalence under arbitrary binding budgets.
