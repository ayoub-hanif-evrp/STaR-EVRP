### B1. Exact one-visit reliability
For a valid fixed plan with precisely one stochastic charging VISIT, let W >= 0 and keep all other travel, service, charging and ready times fixed. Write clock recurrences matching `RouteTiming.evaluate`: travel then ready floor, then wait at station, then start deadline check, then duration, then end deadline check. Propagate a max-plus representation T(w)=max(a+w,b) after the visit (before visit deterministic; use -infinity for absent term). Every finite customer start/depot end constraint T(w)<=d+NUM_TOL gives an upper bound on w from the affine term and a deterministic feasibility condition from the constant term. Intersection yields exact feasible waiting interval [0,tau*] or empty, with tau* possibly infinite. Derive both forward max-plus calculation and independent backward latest-time/monotone check; handle no downstream deadlines, tight/zero/negative caps, already infeasible zero-wait plan, positive wait support, sigma=0 and very large exponents without overflow, correct equality conventions. Explain difference between exact cap and current `isolated_caps`, which drops downstream ready floors and adds a cushion. The bound must be derived for the actual recurrence; do not assert it is strict or loose on every plan. If they coincide under model assumptions, establish why; if not, quantify with verified examples. Frozen station windows off; if a theorem is generalized to station windows, explicitly derive and test extra deadlines or restrict the claim. No claim of arbitrary stochastic travel, stochastic battery or queues.

For nondegenerate lognormal W with finite positive tau*, reliability F_W(tau*)=Phi((ln(tau*)-mu)/sigma); handle zero, infinite, empty and deterministic cases separately. Verify that equality boundary is treated consistently with numeric tolerance. For 0 visits, reliability is deterministic 0 or 1, completion deterministic, no MC needed; do not imply source no-charge routes were newly optimized. For multiple visits, one-dimensional identity does not apply.

Derive exact unconditional expected full return where possible: for final T(w)=max(a+w,b), E[T]=a+E[W]+E[(b-a-W)_+] or b+E[(W-(b-a))_+] (when b finite). For k=b-a>0, lognormal upper stop-loss E[(W-k)_+]=exp(mu+sigma^2/2) Phi((mu+sigma^2-ln k)/sigma)-k Phi((mu-ln k)/sigma). For k<=0 or absent b, E[T]=a+E[W]. For deterministic sigma=0 use exact clock. Check stable numerical evaluation and nonnegative results; validate against independent quadrature or high-sample MC with SE intervals, not exact equality to empirical means. Completion is unconditional, not conditional on success. If exact mean cannot be made robust for all inputs, document and use reliable numerical integration with error tolerance; do not label numerical approximations exact.

### B2. Minimal charging for a fixed single-visit topology
For a fully charged departure and a single visit, let e_pre and e_post be deterministic energy required before/after the visit. Feasibility requires pre/post segments within capacity, arrival battery B_arr=Q-e_pre, and q in [0,Q-B_arr] with q>=e_post-B_arr (and nonnegative). Thus q_min=max(0,e_post-B_arr) is the smallest feasible quantity, subject to exact frozen tolerances, load/coverage and station behavior. For positive linear rate, increasing q does not improve any deadline or reduce full completion for a fixed topology because all clock recurrences are nondecreasing in q, and waiting law does not depend on q; any later ready floors can make times equal, so weak dominance, not strict. Check frozen trace semantics for q<=NUM_TOL and full departure assumptions. Show theorem only applies to exactly one visit and no downstream stochastic energy/recharge opportunity, not general multi-station allocations or nonlinear/queue-coupled charging. Validate min q and slightly larger q including max charge against frozen simulator. A zero-quantity station visit still incurs a wait under frozen rules; compare zero-visit route separately if allowed and physically feasible.

### B3. Marginal reliability upper bound

No independence needed for the min bound; product bound would need appropriate independence AND simultaneous necessary events, do not reuse legacy proof uncritically. Match the actual backwards code, including cap cushion and tolerance. Explain that true R<=U and U<target is a necessary-condition rejection for the true fixed-plan chance constraint. It does not prove empirical R_hat_512<=U. In frozen STaR the screen is used only when the incumbent sample score is target-attaining (best[1]==1; verify actual score definition), not as an unconditional global pruning oracle. Give an explicit simple reproducible finite-sample counterexample, e.g. true p<0.95 but a sample can exceed 0.95. Do not claim analytical screening preserves all finite-tape search trajectories or certifies global infeasibility/attainment. Handle degenerate wait parameters and no visits. Test against exact one-visit probabilities and multi-visit synthetic fixtures. Discuss QBR sufficient condition under independent visit waits and monotone timing when quantile replay is feasible; not globally optimized and not a sample confidence certificate.
