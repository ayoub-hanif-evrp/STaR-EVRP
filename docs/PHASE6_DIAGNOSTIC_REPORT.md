# Phase 6 route diagnostics

`route_features.csv` contains one row per original CON route in the 120-case roster. Source plans are recovered by case and exact route ID from the Phase 2.5 development baseline or the Phase 3 CON bundle. Each exposed route joins to the c64 Phase 3 or Phase 5 plan and validation records, with the raw and selected plan hashes retained. No-charge routes contribute population counts and source-only features. The existing ten-method c64 denominator reconciled with the Phase 5 archive and flat CSV.

Distances use the frozen matrix; detour is original minus customer-only distance. Depot slack is the recorded closing time minus the deterministic end clock, and minimum customer slack uses zero-wait service starts. Isolated wait caps are the frozen screen's conservative caps. Local alternatives count the up-to-three nearest replacements at each current station stop for which both adjacent legs can individually fit a full battery. This is not a claim that the entire route remains feasible after replacement. The energy lower bound uses the customer-only Euclidean route and maximum capacity per segment. It may be loose. Distinct stations are counted per route. Actual zero charge, small positive charge with zero billed duration and strictly greater charge are separate counts.

Use the pre-repair features to compare complexity, minimum slack, source reliability and length. Features beginning with `selected_` and topology or quantity changes are post-selection descriptions. The family and quartile patterns cannot separate route length, accumulated waits, station availability and slack. The analysis labels point-only Wilson failures separately, otherwise classifies the most frequent timing failure type on the route. Scenario failure counts remain available for all exposed routes.

`paired_directions.csv` records signed gains and losses and their shares of the total route mean. `attainment_transitions.csv` contains all four matched Wilson cells. `mechanism_strata.csv` includes route and parent denominators, parent-equal means and intervals where there are at least eight nonempty parent blocks. `parent_influence.csv` retains every observed parent effect and the leave-one-parent-out pooled estimate. The registered `R2_10_2/A/0` case and additional lexicographically selected observed cases are separately labeled in `diagnostic_cases.csv`.
